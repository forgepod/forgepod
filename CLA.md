## Why it exists

The project is AGPL-3.0 and intends to stay that way. This agreement lets the project
also distribute your contribution under other terms, which is what makes a commercial
licence possible for organisations that cannot accept AGPL.

Saying that plainly is deliberate. A project that keeps the option quiet and then uses
it is the version of this that makes people angry.
